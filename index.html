06 / GOOD TO KNOW

Good to
know.

Do I need an appointment or can I walk in?

Walk-ins are always welcome, but booking ahead guarantees your chair and cuts your wait time.

What's your cancellation policy?

We ask for at least 24 hours notice so we can offer your slot to someone else.

Do you take walk-ins for kids' cuts?

Yes, though booking ahead is recommended on weekends when we're busiest.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash, all major cards, and contactless/Apple Pay.

How early should I arrive for my first visit?

A few minutes early is plenty — we'll have you in the chair close to your booked time.
